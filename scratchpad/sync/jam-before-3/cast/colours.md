## ColourNames

```
@brief Named colour palette — name to ARGB word.

Maps every named colour in the framework's palette to its packed `uint32_t`
ARGB word. The key is the colour name resolved from a stylesheet or theme;
the value is the opaque colour word the renderer wraps into a `juce::Colour`
at the read site.
```
